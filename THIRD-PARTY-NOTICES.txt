Numeris — Third-Party Notices
=============================

This file is the license audit trail required by the Numeris engineering
specification (§5.1): every production dependency is recorded here, checked at
the exact version used, with its license and notice obligations. Full license
texts for the licenses referenced below are kept in the LICENSES/ directory.

Numeris itself (the crates numeris-core, numeris-stats, numeris-command and
numeris-project, plus the desktop application) is distributed under the
Numeris Software License — see the LICENSE file at the repository root.

                of the statistical engine crates.

Note: the Numeris statistical core (numeris-core, numeris-stats) is an
independent, pure-Rust implementation. Linear algebra (QR decomposition),
statistical distributions (Normal/t/F/chi-square) and CSV parsing (RFC 4180)
are implemented in-repo and contain NO third-party statistical or numerical
code (no statrs, no faer, no nalgebra, no Polars, no DuckDB).
